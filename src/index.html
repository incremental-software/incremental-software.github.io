Open standards

We don't just build on the platform. We help shape it.

Simeon has worked directly on multiple major web browsers and is a founding member of the WebExtensions Community Group, the venue where browser engineers from all major browsers and extension developers debate and agree on the future of cross-browser extensions.

When a platform gap is blocking your product, we can do more than work around it. We can take it to the group, create proposals, build consensus, and contribute changes directly to browser engines.
