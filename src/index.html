WHAT WE DO

Four things,
done well.

We take on a small number of engagements at a time. If what you need isn't one of these, ask anyway — the answer is often "we know who to call."

01

Platform consulting

Deep technical work on your extension: architecture reviews, MV3 migrations, performance, security posture, platform API questions that nobody else has answers to.

Typical engagement · 2–6 weeks
02

Review & store navigation

Help preparing, submitting, and when necessary appealing reviews on the Chrome Web Store, Firefox Add-ons, and Edge Add-ons. We've been on both sides of the process.

Typical engagement · Ad-hoc or retainer
03

Standards advocacy

Represent your extension's interests in the WebExtensions Working Group. Draft proposals, navigate cross-browser politics, turn product needs into platform features.

Typical engagement · Retainer
04

Diligence & audits

Acquirer-side and investor-side technical due diligence on extension businesses. Codebase assessment, platform risk, review-process exposure, maintainer health.

Typical engagement · 1–3 weeks
