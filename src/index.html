THE APPROACH

Small steps. Real expertise. No account managers.

Most extension problems don't need a team. They need one person who has genuinely seen the edge case before, and who can say what's worth doing and what isn't.

Incremental Software is deliberately a practice of one. When you hire us, you're hiring Simeon Vincent — co-chair of the W3C WebExtensions Community Group, formerly Developer Advocate for Chrome extensions at Google and for add-ons at Mozilla. No pod, no strategy deck, no handoff.

How an engagement starts

A 30-minute call. You describe what you're building, what's stuck, and what "done" would look like. We'll tell you whether we're the right fit within a day, and if we are, scope a two-to-six-week engagement with a fixed fee.

What you get

A written technical brief, as much pairing time as the problem needs, code reviews, and where relevant, direct outreach to the people at Google, Mozilla, Apple, and Microsoft who can move your issue along. What you don't get: deliverables-as-slide-decks.

Most of our work is under NDA and doesn't appear on this site. The selected work section is a small, permissioned sample.
